Your goal is to create the necessary spare parts to repair your spaceship.
This objective is visible on the spaceship, to complete it, you must use
conveyors to insert the required parts into it.

To create these components, you need to build a factory that automatically
mines and refines raw resources, such as iron, in assembly machines.

Buildings can be constructed by pressing the B key, which switches you 
into top-down build mode. Buildings can be selected either by pressing
the number keys 1–5 or by clicking on buildings in your inventory. 
The inventory can be opened by pressing the E key.

By pressing Backspace or the corresponding building number, you can cancel your building selection.
If you do not have a building selected in build mode, you can click on already 
built structures to open their container.
From a building’s container, you can take products, or if the building 
uses recipes, you can assign them by clicking on them.

Buildings accept items from conveyors or other buildings from any side, 
but they only output items from the side marked in red, which is visible when placing the building.
The “Splitter” building can have up to three output points.

You can take products from a building by opening its container and double-clicking on the product. 
You can also craft items from your inventory, such as conveyors, if you have the required ingredients.

WASD – movement
E – inventory
ESC – close menu
SPACE – jump
B – toggle build mode
Left mouse click – build
Right mouse click – remove building
R – rotate clockwise
Numbers 1–5 – select building (can also be selected from inventory)
Backspace – cancel building selection
F4 – exit game

Debug:
Page Up – populate containers
Page Down – infinite building materials
